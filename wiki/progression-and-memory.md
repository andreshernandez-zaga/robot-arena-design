# Progression & Memory

## Limited memory (slots)

- You can hold only a limited number of blueprints.
- Flow:
  1. Capture a blueprint into a slot.
  2. Cross blueprints held in slots.
  3. Decide where the result goes: into the captured blueprint's slot, or replacing one of your robots' blueprints.
- Example with three slots: your original, the captured one, the remix.
- As you play more, you have to drop designs to make room. Those choices shape your identity over your lifetime as a designer.

## Rewards by outcome

- **Winners** get more access, with few restrictions (e.g. one full blueprint, or seeing two designs).
- **Losers** get less (e.g. one design, or part of one), but playing should always be rewarded.
- Losers may improve with high likelihood from what they do see, since their squad is likely weaker than the winner's.
- Winners improving faster is accepted.
- **Possible:** pay currency to see more (see risks).

## Limits on crossing

- The number of crossings is limited, so each one has weight.
