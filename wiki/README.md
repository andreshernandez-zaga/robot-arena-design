# Robot Arena — Design Wiki

> Working title. Nothing here is final. This wiki captures the concept as it stands so it can be refined over time.

## One-line concept

Players design robot squads, test them against each other in asynchronous turn-based matches, and evolve their designs by crossing their own blueprints with those of opponents they have fought.

**Core sentence:** *You select by phenotype; you inherit genotype.*
You choose what to cross based on how a robot looks and how it fought. What you actually get is its hidden blueprint.
